1. Browser storage

The client can use local or per-tab browser storage for preferences and continuity. Current preference keys are poorup.theme.id.v2 (theme), poorup.music.enabled.v1 (global music switch), poorup.music.preferences (approved track, volume, shuffle, loop, and theme mode), and poorup.music.position.v1 (one of four corner positions). These are local browser preferences, not account credentials.

Theme defaults are selected automatically when a theme changes. A custom track, shuffle, or loop choice is stored as a custom choice until the next theme change resets it to that theme's default and enables loop.

2. Session continuity

Session data lets a player return to a seat after an ordinary reload or short disconnect. Never share a session token. The operator must publish the final token lifetime, revocation behavior, storage choice, and recovery process before describing sessions as secure credentials.

3. Essential versus optional choices

Preferences required to render a chosen theme or reconnect a room are different from optional analytics or music choices. Music is optional: browsers may reject autoplay, in which case the selected track remains ready and the player asks for a click to start. Clearing browser data removes these local choices and restores the theme soundtrack defaults.

4. Game telemetry

Telemetry supports balancing and reliability work through bounded event names, coarse values, board and ruleset dimensions, bot mode, provider status, and scoped pseudonyms. It excludes raw names, account identifiers, room codes, chat, tokens, hidden cards, opponent secrets, and private loan terms.

5. Analytics console

The operator console uses minimum-cohort suppression, explicit denominators, versioned read models, no-store responses, and association labels. It is intended for internal aggregate analysis, not player profiling. The operator must confirm who may access it and how long source data is retained.

6. Your controls

Players can clear browser storage through their browser settings. Account deletion, export, and revocation controls will be enabled only after the operator confirms identity verification, retention, backup aging, and live-room consequences. Questions belong in the Privacy or Support notice, not public chat.
