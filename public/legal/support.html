1. Start here

Check the Rules page and the current room message first. If a round is active, do not reload repeatedly or open duplicate tabs while a payment, auction, contract, or reconnect decision is pending. Include your approximate time zone and the smallest useful reproduction when contacting Support.

2. Gameplay bug reports

Describe the room phase, the action you attempted, what the server showed, and what you expected. A screenshot is useful; credentials, session tokens, private chat, and hidden opponent information are not. Do not publish a working exploit in a public room. The final page will list the approved reporting address and expected response target.

3. Security reports

Report suspected account takeover, session replay, data exposure, rate-limit bypass, or server-authority issues privately. Include a safe reproduction and impact, then stop testing. Do not access another player’s account, delete data, or retain more information than needed to demonstrate the issue. The operator must publish a security contact and emergency escalation path before launch.

4. Account access and recovery

For a lost session, explain the account username and approximate time without sending a password or bearer token. Account deletion, export, and session revocation will become available only after the operator confirms identity verification, retention, backup aging, and the effect on active rooms. Guest play remains account-free.

5. Privacy and data requests

Use the privacy contact named in the Privacy notice for access, correction, deletion, export, restriction, or objection requests. Do not put personal data in chat or a public room. The final process must state identity verification, response timing, exceptions, and how a request affects a live match and backups.

6. Accessibility feedback

Tell us which page, viewport, input method, assistive technology, or motion setting caused difficulty. Poorup aims to keep headings, skip links, keyboard focus, contrast, reduced motion, forced colors, and touch targets predictable. Feedback helps us distinguish a visual preference from a blocker.

7. Service status and maintenance

During maintenance, new rooms and new rounds can be paused while active rounds drain where safe. The final operator will publish the status location, incident history, planned windows, and recovery communication channel. Never rely on a social post as the only record of a service incident.
