Privacy notice

This notice explains what the Poorup browser game can process, why it is needed, and how players control visibility.

FACT REVIEW REQUIRED. This draft does not identify a legal entity or promise a retention period. Those facts are intentionally withheld rather than guessed.

1. Controller and contact

Poorup is an online board-game service. The legal name, postal address, and privacy contact for the operator will be published here before this notice becomes effective. Use the Support notice for the current draft escalation path.

2. Scope

This notice applies to the Poorup website, browser client, public rooms, private rooms, profiles, social features, rankings, game sessions, support requests, and optional bot-advisor features. A third-party website linked from Poorup has its own notice.

3. Data we process

Account and profile data

If you create an account, the service can process a username, display name, password verifier, player colour, avatar grid, account preferences, achievements, rankings, and match history. Passwords are processed as verifiers and are not intended to be readable by staff.

Guest and session data

Guest play uses a per-tab client identifier, a chosen alias, room membership, and the session information needed to reconnect a seat. Browser preferences such as theme, sound, music, and local patrol progress can be stored locally.

Gameplay and social data

Rooms process board state, turn actions, ownership, contracts, loans, market and casino actions, chat, invitations, friendships, blocks, and public profile settings. Visibility settings control which profile and history fields other players may see.

Technical and support data

The server may receive network address information, browser and transport details, timestamps, error information, and the contents of a support or security report. Exact logging fields and retention are fact-dependent and must be confirmed by the operator.

Telemetry

Balancing telemetry is designed to contain coarse event names, bounded values, board and ruleset dimensions, and scoped pseudonyms. It must not contain passwords, tokens, raw room codes, chat, hidden cards, opponent secrets, or private loan terms.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Processing is needed to authenticate accounts, seat players, run the rules, settle a round, prevent abuse, restore a disconnected session, provide social features, answer support requests, secure the service, and measure aggregate game health. The operator must map each purpose to the applicable Germany/EU legal basis before this notice is effective. Optional analytics and external AI processing must not be silently treated as essential.

5. Public visibility and player controls

Guest identity is local to a browser session. Account users can choose profile and history visibility where the client offers that setting. Public rankings use display information intended for that surface; private match details, account identifiers, session tokens, hidden cards, and private contract terms are not public profile data.

A privacy setting can make history private or limited to accepted friends. A stranger must not receive recent matches or stable internal account identifiers from a public card.

6. Aggregate analytics

The operator console is intended for aggregate balancing and reliability analysis. It uses minimum-cohort suppression, scoped pseudonyms, bounded dimensions, explicit denominators, and “association, not causation” labels. Analytics pages are not a second player directory and must never expose raw identity or private gameplay details.

7. Service providers and sharing

Data may be processed by the hosting provider, the realtime transport infrastructure, storage and backup services, error-monitoring tools, and an external AI provider only when that feature is enabled and the operator has approved it. The operator must name each provider, purpose, location, transfer mechanism, and deletion commitment in the effective version.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect the service and players, or to handle a security incident. Poorup is not designed to sell player data.

8. Retention and deletion

The current implementation uses bounded histories and stores rather than a final legal retention schedule. The effective notice must state how long account, session, social, match, support, security, backup, and telemetry records remain, what deletion means for a live round, and how backups age out.

9. Your rights and requests

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, export, restriction, objection, or information about processing. The operator will publish the responsible contact and identity-verification process before enabling account deletion or export controls. Requests should not be sent through public chat.

10. Children and age

The operator must set and publish the minimum age for the service, explain whether parental consent is required, and define how an underage account is handled. Poorup must not knowingly retain children’s personal data beyond what the confirmed policy permits.

11. Changes and contact

The effective version will show an effective date, last-updated date, version identifier, and a short change summary. Material changes will be communicated through the channel and notice period required by the applicable law. Questions and complaints go to the operator contact listed above and, where applicable, the competent supervisory authority.
