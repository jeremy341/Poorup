Privacy & Account Data

A plain-language description of the data Poorup stores for account play, guest play, social features, and aggregate operations.

Last updated: 17 September 2026 · This page describes the current local server behavior.

Collected dataGuest playYour controlsRetentionSecurity

What is collected

An account stores a username, display name, avatar and colour choices, password verifier, optional recovery-email status, preferences, completed-round statistics, achievements, and account-linked match summaries. Passwords, bearer tokens, and provider credentials are never shown in an export.

Guest play

You can create or join a table without an account. Guest identity is kept in the browser for the current play experience; the server does not create a personal account history for guest rounds. Only privacy-safe aggregate operational metrics may remain.

Your account controls

From Profile, a signed-in player can request an owner-safe JSON export, revoke other sessions, set and verify a recovery email, or request account deletion. Deletion first deactivates the account for 30 days. During that period the Profile surface can cancel the request or download data. A seated player must leave the table before requesting deletion.

After the grace period, account-linked profile, social, cosmetic, achievement, and personal match-history data are removed or anonymized. Existing references use the label ACCOUNT DEACTIVATED. Aggregate analytics cannot be used to reconstruct an account and may be retained for up to 12 months.

Social and match projections

Friends, requests, invites, notifications, and match projections are account-linked while the account is active. Private match details are shown only to the authorized owner or an allowed relationship. Deactivated accounts are omitted from search, rankings, invites, and new social actions.

AI provider boundary

Bot-provider configuration is an operator feature. Player passwords, session cookies, private chat, private deal terms, and raw account identifiers are not sent to an AI provider. Aggregate bot outcomes may be recorded for balancing.

Recovery and security

Recovery links are single-use and expire after 30 minutes. A verified recovery-email change keeps the previous address active until the new address is verified. Cookies are intended to be HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite-limited in production. Mail delivery and operator ownership remain deployment configuration rather than facts asserted by this page.

Questions or account help

For support, use Poorup Issues. Do not include passwords, recovery tokens, session cookies, or private game details in a public issue.
